# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how this education flashcards utility ("the App") handles information.

## Information We Collect

The App stores study data locally on your device using on-device storage (UserDefaults), including:

- Flashcards and topics you create
- Quiz history and scores
- Learning progress statistics
- Achievement unlock state
- Onboarding completion flag

The App does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal identity information.

## How We Use Information

Local data is used solely to provide flashcard study features, progress tracking, achievements, and app settings.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Because study content remains on your device, it is not transmitted to our servers by the App itself.

## Third-Party Services

If you choose Rate Us, the operating system may present an App Store review prompt governed by Apple’s policies.

## Data Retention & Deletion

You can erase all locally stored content at any time using Reset All Data in Settings.

## Contact

For privacy questions, visit https://example.com/privacy-policy

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
